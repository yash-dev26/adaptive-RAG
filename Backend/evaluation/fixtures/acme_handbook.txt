# Acme Corp Employee Handbook (Fixture Document)

This is a synthetic fixture document used only by the evaluation suite in
this folder. It is not a real policy document. Facts below are made up
specifically so they can be checked exactly (see cases/golden_set.json).

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 22 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar
year, credited in full at the start of January. PTO does not roll over
between years; unused days above 5 are forfeited on December 31st. New
hires who join after July 1st receive a prorated 11 days for their first
year. PTO requests must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance
through the HR portal, except for emergency leave, which can be requested
retroactively within 48 hours.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without prior approval.
Fully remote arrangements (5 days per week) require written sign-off from
both the employee's manager and HR, and are reviewed every 6 months.
Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours, 10am to
4pm in their local time zone. Equipment stipends for remote setup are
capped at $500 per employee, reimbursed once per 24-month period.

## Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses under $75 can be submitted with a receipt photo through
the mobile app and are typically reimbursed within 3 business days.
Expenses of $75 or more require a manager's approval before submission.
Travel expenses (flights, hotels) must be booked through the corporate
travel portal to qualify for reimbursement; expenses booked independently
are reimbursed at 50% of the standard rate.

## Notice Period

Employees who resign are asked to provide 2 weeks of written notice.
Employees in engineering leadership roles (Staff+ and above) are asked to
provide 4 weeks of notice to allow for knowledge transfer. Acme reserves
the right to end employment immediately in lieu of notice, in which case
the employee is paid for the notice period.

## Data & Security Policy

All company laptops must have disk encryption enabled and are enrolled in
mobile device management (MDM) automatically on first login. Employees
must report a lost or stolen device to IT within 1 hour of noticing it is
missing. Production database access requires two-factor authentication
and is logged; access is reviewed quarterly by the security team.
